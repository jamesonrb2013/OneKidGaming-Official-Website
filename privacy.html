Privacy matters.

This page explains the basic approach for the public OneKidGaming website. Product-specific services may have additional privacy terms.

External services

Links to Discord, Roblox, and other third-party services are governed by those providers' own privacy policies and terms.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@onekidgaming.blitz.cloud.
