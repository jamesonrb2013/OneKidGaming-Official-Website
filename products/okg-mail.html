About this initiative

OKG Mail is part of the broader effort to provide connected digital services for the OKG community and organization.

Areas of focus

  • Clear mailbox ownership and account access.
  • Reliable sending and receiving workflows.
  • Careful permission handling for shared mailboxes.
  • Consistent integration with future OKG account services.
